## Two different Settings

Settings shows a different set of tabs depending on your role. A brand configures the workspace; a creator configures themselves.

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Brand" icon="building-2">
    Profile, General, Integrations, Royalty Terms, Billing, Notifications
  </Card>
  <Card title="Creator" icon="users">
    Profile, Payouts, Notifications
  </Card>
</Columns>

## Profile

Your name, photo and account security. Both roles have it and it is the same page for each, because it is about you rather than about the workspace.

## General

Brand only. Workspace name, URL slug, logo, and the **payout currency** creators are paid in. Twenty currencies are supported, including USD, AUD, GBP, EUR, CAD and NZD.

<Callout kind="warning">
  The payout currency is what earnings are denominated in. A pay run refuses a set of earnings that does not match it, so changing it on a workspace with live balances needs thought rather than a quick edit.
</Callout>

Deleting the workspace lives here too, under its own heading.

## Integrations

Brand only, and the only place integrations are connected. None of them are part of onboarding.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Meta" icon="megaphone">
    Connect your Business account and choose the ad account this workspace publishes into and reads from.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Shopify" icon="shopping-cart">
    Link the store whose catalogue creators request product from.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Two cards appear underneath once the integration they belong to is connected:

- **Gifting policy**, once Shopify is connected. The limits on what creators can request. See [Gifting](/gifting#setting-limits).
- **Adset templates**, once Meta is connected. Saved adset configurations you can build an ad into when publishing, instead of duplicating an existing adset every time.

Klaviyo and Slack appear at the bottom as placeholders. Neither does anything yet.

## Royalty Terms

Brand only. The default terms every new creator inherits, and any alternative sets you keep. Covered in full in [Royalties and payouts](/royalties-and-payouts#royalty-terms).

## Billing

Brand only. Your plan, what it allows, and what you are using against it. See [Plans and limits](/plans).

Automatic pay runs are configured here as well as on the Payouts page, since a saved payment method is a billing concern as much as a payouts one.

## Payouts

Creator only. Two cards:

- **Stripe Connect**, where a creator onboards to the account their money will land in. Nothing can be withdrawn until this is finished.
- **Automatic cash outs**, a switch and a threshold. It cannot be turned on without a payout method, and says so rather than failing when a creator tries.

## Notifications

Both roles. Three email toggles, each covering the in-app notifications of the same kind:

| Toggle | Covers |
|---|---|
| New messages | Somebody sent you a message |
| Content updates | Approvals, rejections, revision requests and ad links |
| Payout updates | Payout requests, approvals and transfers |

Turning an email off does not turn off the in-app notification.
