## Overview

Upload user-generated content (UGC) to manage assets for Meta Ads.

## Steps

1. Go to your Hotline UGC dashboard.
2. Click "Upload Content".
3. Select your video or image files.
4. Confirm and wait for processing.
5. Uploaded items appear in your asset list.

## Supported formats
- .mp4, .mov for video
- .jpg, .png for images

<Callout kind="tip">
Check file size limits: max 200MB per file.
</Callout>
