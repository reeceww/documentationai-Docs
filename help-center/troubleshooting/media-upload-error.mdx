## Symptoms

- The upload stalls part way and does not finish
- The file is rejected as soon as you choose it
- The upload appears to complete but nothing shows in the Hub

## Check these first

<Steps>
  <Step title="File size" icon="scale">

  The limit is **500 MB**. A long export at a high bitrate passes it easily.

  Re-export at 1080p rather than 4K. Meta downscales ad creative anyway, so this costs nothing you will see in the ad.

  </Step>

  <Step title="File format" icon="file-video">

  Video must be `.mp4`, `.mov` or `.webm`. Images must be `.jpg`, `.png`, `.webp` or `.gif`.

  A `.mov` exported from an iPhone is fine. A `.hevc` or `.avi` is not, and re-exporting as `.mp4` is the quickest fix.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Connection" icon="wifi">

  A large file on an unstable connection can stall near the end. If the progress bar reaches high nineties and stops, retry on a different network before assuming the file is at fault.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Still failing

<Callout kind="info">
  Email [hello@hotlineugc.com](mailto:hello@hotlineugc.com) with the file name, its size, and roughly when you tried. That is enough to find the attempt in the logs and tell you what actually stopped it.
</Callout>
