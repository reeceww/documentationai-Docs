## Overview

Push UGC assets from Hotline UGC to Meta Ads in a few steps.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect Meta Ads Account">
    Go to Settings → Integrations.
    Click "Connect Meta Ads" and follow prompts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select UGC for Sync">
    On your asset list, check the assets you want to use.
    Click "Sync to Meta Ads".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete Sync">
    Wait for the sync confirmation.
    Assets will now be available in your Meta Ads library.
  </Step>
</Steps>
