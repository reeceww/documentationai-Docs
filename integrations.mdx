## Overview

Hotline connects to three services today. Each one is optional except Meta, which the core loop depends on.

All three are connected from **Settings**, not during onboarding, and in whatever order you like. See [Settings](/settings#integrations) for what else lives on that tab.

## Meta

The one integration the product does not work without. It publishes your approved videos and reads back what they did.

### What it can do

Hotline creates ads in your ad account **paused**. Nothing it publishes can spend money until a person turns it on in Meta.

It reads spend, impressions, clicks, reach, video plays, purchases and purchase value for the ads it created, every two hours, and attaches them to the video and creator behind each ad.

### Permissions requested

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| `ads_read` | Read performance for the ads Hotline created |
| `ads_management` | Create the ad, its creative and its adset |
| `business_management` | Resolve which ad accounts you have access to |
| `pages_read_engagement`, `pages_show_list` | Identify the Page an ad runs from |
| `instagram_basic` | Read the linked Instagram account |
| `instagram_branded_content_ads_brand` | Run partnership ads under a creator's handle, when enabled |

<Callout kind="info">
  Your ad account, pixel and audiences stay yours. Hotline never sets a budget, never changes a bid, and never turns an ad on.
</Callout>

### Connecting

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings" icon="settings">
    Find the Meta card in **Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorise" icon="shield">
    Sign in to Meta and grant access. Revoking access later leaves ads already published running; they simply stop reporting back into Hotline.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the ad account" icon="link">
    Pick the ad account this workspace publishes into and reads from.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Shopify

Connect a store and Hotline mirrors your product catalogue, so creators can ask for the products they need to film with.

### What it can do

<Callout kind="warning">
  Hotline **writes to your store**. It creates a draft order at a 100% discount and completes it, which is how a gift reaches a creator. That is the only order it ever creates, and no payment method is used.
</Callout>

| Scope | Why |
|---|---|
| `read_products` | Mirror the catalogue creators choose from |
| `read_inventory` | Avoid offering something out of stock |
| `read_orders`, `read_fulfillments` | Track a gift parcel through to delivery |
| `write_draft_orders` | Create the gift order itself |

Hotline does not read your customer records and stores no Shopify customer identifier. A creator's name, email and shipping address are supplied by the creator and passed to Shopify only so the order can be shipped to them.

### Connecting

Install Hotline UGC from the Shopify App Store onto your store, then link it to your workspace when the app prompts you. Once linked, sync your products from the app's setup section and the catalogue appears under **Hub**.

## Stripe

Creator payouts run on **Stripe Connect**.

Creators onboard to Stripe themselves, so the money lands in an account they own and control. Hotline never holds creator funds: a pay run charges the brand first and pays creators only once that payment settles.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect the workspace" icon="link">
    Open the Stripe card in **Settings** and connect your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Creators onboard" icon="user-check">
    Each creator completes Stripe onboarding from their own Earnings page. Until they do, a balance can accrue but cannot be paid out.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run a pay run" icon="dollar-sign">
    From **Payouts**, pay everyone whose balance has cleared its hold period and reached the minimum, or put pay runs on a schedule and let them charge a saved payment method.
  </Step>
</Steps>
