## Overview

Hotline covers the whole life of a creator video: it arrives, gets reviewed, becomes an ad, earns money, and pays somebody. Each stage has a place in the product.

## Content review

Creators upload into the Hub. Every upload is a version, including the first.

**Notes are pinned to a timestamp** on the video, so feedback points at the frame it is about rather than describing it. When a creator answers a revision request, the new cut arrives as the next version. The previous file stays reachable, and unresolved notes are carried onto the new version with a pointer back to the frame they were drawn on.

<Callout kind="info">
  The drawing stays with the frame it was drawn on. Copying an annotation onto a different cut with its coordinates intact would leave the arrow pointing at something that moved, so only the note travels.
</Callout>

## Publishing to Meta

An approved video goes to Meta with **Upload to Meta**. You choose the ad account and ad name, and where the ad should sit: duplicate an existing adset, drop it into an existing one, or build one from a saved template.

Ads are created **paused**. Nothing spends until somebody turns it on in Meta.

Bulk upload takes several approved creatives in one pass, which is the normal way to ship a batch of variants.

## Performance and attribution

Meta sends spend, impressions, clicks, reach, video plays, purchases and purchase value back against each individual ad, every two hours. Because each ad is mapped to the video and the creator behind it, the numbers attach to a person rather than to a campaign.

A creative running as several ads can be read either way:

- **Individual**, one card per ad
- **Aggregated**, one card per video with a variant count and combined figures

Where Meta provides incremental attribution, iROAS uses it. Where it does not, the card shows an estimate and says so rather than presenting a modelled figure as measured.

<Callout kind="warning">
  Creators see impressions, clicks, CTR and earnings for their own videos only. Account wide spend, ROAS and other creators on the roster are never shown to them.
</Callout>

## Royalties and payout models

Every creator has one base model, set per creator or inherited from the workspace default.

| Model | How it prices a day |
|---|---|
| Percent of revenue | A share of the revenue that creator's ad brought in |
| Flat fee per order | A fixed amount for every order the ad drove |
| Meta performance | A rate against delivery, measured per 1,000 impressions, per click, or as a percent of ad spend |

Meta performance pays on delivery rather than sales, so a creator earns from a video even when it sells nothing.

Three things layer on top of whichever base is set:

- **Submission fee**, paid once when a video is approved
- **Retainer**, a guaranteed floor per period when a creator hits a volume goal, topping up the difference if royalties fall short
- **Bonus**, paid straight away and outside the payout cap

### Caps and freezing

A **payout cap** is the most a single video can ever earn. It is shared across every ad running that video, so duplicating a creative into four adsets does not multiply what it can pay.

Terms are **frozen onto a video when it is approved**. Editing a creator's terms later reprices new work and never touches work already approved, so nobody's agreed rate changes retroactively.

## Payouts

Earnings accrue nightly. A balance becomes available after the hold period, and then appears in **Payouts**.

A pay run charges the brand the creators' royalties plus payment processing at cost, and pays each creator through **Stripe Connect** into an account the creator owns and onboarded to themselves. Hotline collects from the brand first and pays creators only once that settles, so it never fronts the money and never keeps any of it.

Creators can also cash out themselves once they clear the minimum, and can turn on automatic cash outs at a threshold they choose.

## Gifting

With Shopify connected, creators request products from your real catalogue, or you send one directly without waiting to be asked.

An approved request creates a **draft order at a 100% discount** in your store and completes it. Nothing is charged and no payment method is used. When you fulfil the order, tracking flows back and the shipment is tracked through to delivery inside Hotline.
