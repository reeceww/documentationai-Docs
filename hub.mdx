## What the Hub is

The Hub is where the work lives. Everything to do with making a video, from the reference material that inspires it through to the finished cut and the product a creator needed to film it, sits behind one nav entry and a row of tabs.

The tabs differ by role. A brand and a creator open the same Hub and see a different set, because some of it is yours to run and some of it is theirs to use.

| Tab | Brand | Creator |
|---|---|---|
| Boards | Yes | Yes |
| Pages | Yes | Yes |
| Discovery | Yes | No |
| Content | Yes, as **Content** | Yes, as **My Content** |
| Gifting | Yes | No |
| Products | No | Yes |

<Callout kind="info">
  Brands manage products from **Gifting**, which is why there is no separate Products tab on their side. Creators have no Gifting tab because requesting product is what the Products tab is for.
</Callout>

## Boards

A board collects ads and creatives into one place: references, a mood board, a set of variants you want to talk about, the do and do not list for a campaign.

Boards can be shared two ways:

- **With people in the workspace**, by naming them
- **By link**, with anyone at all, including people who have no Hotline account

A share link can carry a password and an expiry date. Both are optional and both are worth setting when the link is going outside your company.

## Pages

Pages is a block editor for anything written: briefs, guidelines, company documents, the detail that does not fit on a board.

Each page has a visibility:

| Visibility | Who sees it |
|---|---|
| Private | Only you, plus anyone you share it with individually |
| Team | Owners and admins |
| Creators | Owners, admins and the creators in the workspace |

Sharing a page with one person individually works on top of the visibility setting, so a private page can go to a single creator without opening it to everybody.

Pages can be pinned to the top of the list, up to five at a time, and marked complete when a brief has been used. Completed pages drop below a divider rather than disappearing.

## Discovery

Discovery is a brand-side research tool built on the Meta Ad Library. It is for finding ads worth learning from, and saving them somewhere you can talk about them.

<Columns cols={3}>
  <Card title="Search" icon="search">
    Search the Ad Library by keyword, product or brand. Filter by country, ad status and media type, and sort the results.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Following" icon="rss">
    The latest ads from brands you have chosen to follow.
  </Card>
  <Card title="For You" icon="sparkles">
    Recommendations drawn from what you have been searching, saving and opening.
  </Card>
</Columns>

Any ad in any of the three views can be saved to a board, which is how a competitor's creative ends up in the same place as the brief that answers it.

<Callout kind="info">
  Discovery searches public ad data. It is not creator discovery: Hotline has no roster to browse and does not source creators for you. You bring the people you want to work with.
</Callout>

## Content

Content is the review queue. Creators upload here, brands watch and respond here, and an approved video is published to Meta from here.

Every upload is a version, including the first. Notes can be pinned to a timestamp on the video, so feedback points at the frame it is about. A revision comes back as the next version with the previous file still reachable.

Creators see the same tab as **My Content**, holding their own uploads and nothing else.

## Products

The creator's side of gifting. It holds the catalogue they can request from, any bundles the brand has published, and their own request and order history with tracking.

A creator needs a shipping address on file before they can request anything, which is why it is one of the steps in creator onboarding.
