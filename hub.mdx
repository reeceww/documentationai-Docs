## What the Hub is

The Hub is where the work lives. Everything to do with making a video, from the brief that asks for it through to the finished cut and what the ads told you afterwards, sits behind one nav entry.

The tabs come in three groups, separated by a rule: **the work**, **the reference**, and **the read**.

| Group | Tab | Brand | Creator |
|---|---|---|---|
| The work | Creatives | Yes | Yes, as **My Creatives** |
| | Briefs | Yes | Yes |
| | Gifting | Yes | No |
| | Products | No | Yes |
| The reference | Documents | Yes | Yes |
| The read | Intelligence | Yes | No |

<Callout kind="info">
  Brands manage products from **Gifting**, which is why there is no separate Products tab on their side. Creators have no Gifting tab because requesting product is what Products is for.
</Callout>

## Creatives

The review queue. Creators upload here, brands watch and respond here, and an approved video is published to Meta from here.

Every upload is a version, including the first. Notes can be pinned to a timestamp on the video, so feedback points at the frame it is about. A revision comes back as the next version with the previous file still reachable.

Creators see the same tab as **My Creatives**, holding their own uploads and nothing else.

## Products

The creator's side of gifting. It holds the catalogue they can request from, any bundles the brand has published, and their own request and order history with tracking.

A creator needs a shipping address on file before they can request anything, which is why it is one of the steps in creator onboarding.

## Documents

A block editor for anything written, from guidelines to company documents.

Each document has a visibility:

| Visibility | Who sees it |
|---|---|
| Private | Only you, plus anyone you share it with individually |
| Team | Owners and admins |
| Creators | Owners, admins and the creators in the workspace |

Sharing with one person individually works on top of that setting, so a private document can go to a single creator without opening it to everybody.

Documents can be pinned to the top of the list, up to five at a time, and marked complete when they have served their purpose. Completed ones drop below a divider rather than disappearing.

<Callout kind="info">
  A document is not a brief. Use Documents for the things that stay true across campaigns, and a [brief](/briefs) for a specific ask you want submissions against.
</Callout>
