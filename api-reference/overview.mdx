<Callout kind="alert">
  **A public API is not available yet.** There are no API keys to issue, no documented endpoints, and no `api.hotlineugc.com`. Anything you may have seen describing one was placeholder content and has been removed.
</Callout>

## What this means today

Everything in Hotline is done through the app at [app.hotlineugc.com](https://app.hotlineugc.com). There is no programmatic way to create work, read performance or trigger a payout from outside it.

If you have built something against endpoints from an earlier version of these docs, it was never live and will not start working.

## What does exist

Two integration surfaces are real, and both are configured from **Settings** rather than called directly:

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Meta" icon="megaphone" href="/integrations#meta">
    Hotline reads and writes your Meta ad account on your behalf, once you authorise it.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Shopify" icon="shopping-cart" href="/integrations#shopify">
    Install the app on your store and Hotline mirrors your catalogue and creates gift orders.
  </Card>
</Columns>

Hotline also receives webhooks from Shopify and Stripe. Those are inbound to Hotline and configured by the integrations themselves, so there is nothing for you to call or register.
